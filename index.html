A Taste of Home,
Far from Home

BEKES Nigerian Restaurant was born from a deep love for Nigerian culture and cuisine. We bring the authentic tastes of Lagos, Abuja and beyond to the cosmopolitan shores of Pearl Qatar — every dish handcrafted with traditional recipes passed down through generations.
